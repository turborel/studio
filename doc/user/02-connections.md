# Connections

[Contents](README.md)

This chapter will describe connecting to a Turborel server: the
connection dialog, a connection URI or a service file, TLS and its
modes, SCRAM, several servers, saved connections and the keychain that
holds their passwords, environments and their colours, read-only
connections, and cancelling, ending and reconnecting. Milestones M1 and
M2 fill it.
